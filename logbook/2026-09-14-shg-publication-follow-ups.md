## Identical entity deficits accounted for

[`block-audit.json`](../data/designs/shg/block-audit.json), generated by
[`scripts/shg_block_audit.py`](../scripts/shg_block_audit.py), compares the
published LibreDWG JSON graphs with their ODA DXFs. It covers every drawing
with a reported entity-count deficit: SHG-003, 004, 005, 010, 012, 016, 022
and 023. Names alone are insufficient: the source gives both omitted blocks
the anonymous name `*I`, while surviving anonymous blocks receive numbered
names in DXF. Matching uses the preserved block-record handles.

| Source handle | Identified content | Members omitted from DXF |
|---|---|---|
| `D3` | Sheet border, grid letters A–D and numbers 1–6 | 17 LWPOLYLINE, 20 MTEXT |
| `FC` | Historical title block, including ISO and the printed date 2/11/2020 | 14 LINE, 10 MTEXT |

Their BLOCK/ENDBLK boundaries add two of each entity type. These two blocks
account for the full recorded deficit in each of the eight drawings. The
audit retains member handles, labels, source/export hashes and the complete
block-name mapping. Decoded-content fingerprints match across all eight
copies after removing only index, handle, ownerhandle, size and bitsize;
geometry, text, styling and other references remain in that comparison.

The headers retain insertion backlinks `D6` and `FF`, but those objects are
absent from each parsed source graph. No decoded INSERT/MINSERT refers to
either omitted block. This supports the interpretation of old template
definitions without a decoded live insertion. It does **not** prove that
proprietary proxy data cannot refer to them, nor that their removal is
visually harmless. Source BLOCK_CONTROL still lists their headers. The
archive now identifies what was omitted without changing fidelity status.

All eight catalogue entries and the web explanation link the audit. All 28
records remain `design_only`, and all preview fidelity remains `unverified`.

provenance, content fingerprint sensitivity and regeneration from published
artifacts. Regeneration requires ezdxf and reports an explicit skip reason
in the standard environment. There is still no CI; these are local results.
